original receipt, never a second draw/spend/event. Resume requires authenticated

task. Grace expiry releases network/session retention according to approved
policy, not despawning or resetting combat without a sim-owned rule. Restart
must obey T022's approved policy and never reuse epochs with a missing ledger.
