# T139 — persistent creature state; join and leave (`crpg-sim`)

**Status: proposed, awaits the user's approval.**

## Task
ADR-0029 Follow-on 6 (`docs/adr/0029-kernel-range-and-time-model.md:485`):
persistent creature state outside encounters (stats, pools, effects, tags),
and the journaled join and leave operations of T13 and T14 (`:256-264`),
under T16's rule that every World change is a journaled operation
(`:273-277`). It closes three hand-offs:
- `tasks/T137.md:815-818`: T139 owns non-`Int` stats and creature tags
  outside encounters.
- `tasks/T138.md:602-603` (B31): T138 added no join operation.
- `tasks/T138.md:851-858`: the T14 join rules, and two open decisions. One is
  whether a join into a terminal RT encounter is rejected. The other is how
  `OnTick` pools refresh outside an encounter.

Today no creature state exists outside an encounter. `World`
(`crates/crpg-sim/src/world.rs:68-99`) has no creature store.
`start_encounter` mints one fresh entity per authored participant
(`crates/crpg-sim/src/combat.rs:1946-1947`) and builds its `Combatant` from
the prefab. `end_encounter` strips every combatant (`:3736-3741`), so the
entities stay live but carry no stats, pools, effects or tags.

## Crate(s)
**`crpg-sim` only.** The work is three packages, landed in this order:
T139a, T139b, T139c. Each is one crate. Two companion packages, T139d
(`crpg-server`) and T139e (`crpg-net`, tests only), must land in the same
batch after T139c, because the two new `HistoryEvent` variants break
exhaustive `match`es there ("Companion packages"). **S-set:** the `World`
struct (a new store, a new `Combatant` field), serde shapes, and the
meaning of history version 2. The tick order is not touched. Every package
is serialized and human-reviewed.

## Decision
- **D1. Persistent state is one new `World` store,
  `creatures: ComponentStore<CreatureState>`.** An entity holds a
  `CreatureState` or a `Combatant`, never both. Outside an encounter the
  `CreatureState` is the only copy of the creature. While the creature
  fights, the `Combatant` is the only copy, so the two can never disagree.
  *Rejected:* fields on `EntityMeta` (`world.rs:45-59`), which change every
  entity's bytes and have no absence form. Also rejected: keeping the
  `CreatureState` during a fight, which leaves a stale second health value
  readable through `World::creatures()`.
- **D2. Persistence is opt-in per creature.** A `CreatureState` comes from
  `register_creature`, which instantiates a placement outside any
  encounter. A combatant is *persistent* when it entered the encounter from
  a `CreatureState`. Only persistent combatants become `CreatureState`
  again when they leave or the encounter is released. Combatants that
  `start_encounter` spawned from authored participants behave exactly as
  today. *Rejected:* writing back every combatant at release, as both
  drafts propose. `history_schedule.rs:383` and `surface_schedule.rs:461`
  call `end_encounter`, so this would move four golden files (open
  question 1).
- **D3. Persistent creatures enter an encounter two ways.** At start they
  come in as *members*, through a new `start_encounter_with_members`. Later
  they come in as *joiners*, through `join_encounter`. Both take an
  `Entrant { entity, initiative }`, where `initiative` is the base key the
  host supplies in place of an encounter participant's authored
  `initiative`. Members compete for the first turn as authored participants
  do. *Rejected:* matching a `CreatureState` to an authored participant by
  placement, which cannot bring a party member into another area's
  encounter. Also rejected: a field on `EncounterSpec`, whose literals sit
  in four other crates (T138 D3) (open question 2).
- **D4. Join placement follows T14 with no new scheduling state.**
  - TB, round 0: the joiner is inserted at its key only if its `(key,
    EntityId)` sorts after the active head. Otherwise it waits.
  - TB, round 1 or later: the joiner always waits.
  - A waiting joiner is a combatant with no timeline entry. The next
    rollover inserts it at its key, because `tb_round_start` re-inserts
    every alive combatant from the store (`combat.rs:3261-3269`). A TB→RT
    switch keys it too, because `switch_to_real_time` keys every alive id
    (`:3528-3530`).
  - RT: the joiner is keyed at `elapsed` with `order: None` (T138 B11).

  *Rejected:* a `pending_joiners`/`pending_joins` field on `CombatState`
  (both drafts), which the rollover already makes unnecessary. Also
  rejected: Beam's clamp to the head's key. With an `EntityId` below the
  head's, the joiner would sort first and break "active is the timeline
  head" (`world.rs:600-611`). See open question 3.
- **D5. A terminal encounter refuses joins** with a new `EncounterTerminal`.
  Terminal means `active == None` in TB, and fewer than two alive in RT
  (T138 D15). Terminal stays absorbing, and an encounter journals
  `EncounterEnded` at most once. *Rejected:* revival by join, which would
  journal a second end (open question 4).
- **D6. Nothing happens to a creature outside an encounter.** No pool
  refreshes (`OnTick` included, since no encounter clock exists), no
  effect expires, and `tick` leaves the store alone. At exit, each effect
  attachment is *suspended* as its remaining rounds. At entry it resumes
  as `round + remaining`, if the new encounter's definition has that
  effect, and is dropped otherwise. *Rejected:* stripping effects at exit,
  which loses information and lets a leave/rejoin cleanse a debuff (open
  question 5).
- **D7. Non-`Int` persistent stats are the non-derived `Fixed` values.**
  The prefab's `stats` is a `BTreeMap<String, Fx16_16>`
  (`crates/crpg-data/src/document.rs:117-119`), and data checks
  completeness only for non-derived `Int` and `Fixed`
  (`crates/crpg-data/src/validation.rs:829-835`). `Bool`, `Enum`, `Tags`
  and `Dice` base stats have no per-creature value anywhere, and a derived
  stat of any kind is a formula, re-evaluated rather than stored.
  *Rejected:* Beam's `crpg_rules::StatValue` and Muse's six-kind
  `PersistentStat`, which both persist values that no data can supply
  (open question 6).
- **D8. Tags are always stored on `CreatureState`, and a persistent
  combatant always carries them.** The T137 B19 rule (tags only in
  encounters with a granting effect) still holds for spawned participants,
  so both `srd_lite` goldens stay put. Under D1, a persistent combatant
  that dropped its tags would lose them for good.
- **D9. Join and leave are free functions plus `HistoryWorld` methods, not
  `CombatAction` variants.** A join has no acting combatant, and AI
  enumeration must not offer either one (the T138 D7 reason).
  `legal_actions` and `crpg-script` are unaffected.
- **D10. Leave mirrors `despawn`, but the entity survives.** One
  crate-private helper, `World::remove_combatant_tracked`, settles the
  encounter for both. Removing the active combatant advances; removing
  any other leaves the turn alone; removing the last one releases the
  encounter. The helper is extracted from `despawn_tracked` without
  change. *Rejected:* a second copy of the settle logic.
- **D11. History stays at version 2.** `Joined` and `Left` join the v2
  vocabulary. The D16 lazy, sticky trigger widens to "the staged world
  holds a `CreatureState` or a persistent combatant". *Rejected:* Beam's
  version 3. It moves no golden, as a lazy bump would not, but six
  assertions in three existing test files pin 2 as the newest version or
  3 as unknown: `tests/history.rs:832`, `tests/history_v2.rs:623`, `:764`
  and `:877`, and `tests/history_v2_contract.rs:468` and `:524`. Editing
  them is a stop under `AGENTS.md`, and replay is exact-build (open
  question 7).
- **D12. No existing golden, replay fixture or hash moves.** Every new
  field and store is absent when empty. Every new event fires only on a
  new operation, and the existing start, release and despawn paths produce
  today's bytes when no creature exists. A moved golden is a stop, not a
  re-bless.

## Behaviour
"Alive" and "terminal" keep their T138 meanings. "Head" is
`world.timeline().iter().next()`. A *persistent* combatant has
`persistent() == Some(_)`. Every item is tagged with its package.

### T139a — the store, registration, and life outside encounters
1. **B1 (D1) [a].** `creatures` is the only persistent creature state in
   the crate. No entity holds both a `CreatureState` and a `Combatant`. A
   `CreatureState` holds no timeline entry and is never a participant. A
   world that never registers a creature writes no `creatures` key, so its
   bytes and `state_hash` equal today's.
2. **B2 (shape) [a].** For every `CreatureState`:
   - `max_health >= 1` and `health <= max_health`;
   - `pools` is nonempty, with distinct pool ids;
   - no name repeats across `attributes` and `fixed`;
   - `suspended` is strictly ascending by `effect`, and every entry has
     `remaining_rounds >= 1`;
   - `tags` is strictly ascending (sorted, distinct), and
     `tags.len() <= crpg_rules::MAX_TAGS`.
3. **B3 (registration checks) [a].** `register_creature` checks in this
   order and mutates nothing on failure:
   - **R1** `TooManyStats` if `ruleset.stats.len() > MAX_COMBAT_STATS`.
   - **R2** `AreaMismatch { expected, found: spec.area }` if the world is
     bound and `world.area() != Some(spec.area)`.
   - **R3** `MissingCreature { prefab: spec.placement.prefab }` if
     `spec.creature.id != spec.placement.prefab`.
   - **R4** `read_creature_stats`, with start's per-creature errors in
     start's order: `MissingStat`, `InvalidStatValue` (fractional `Int`, or
     nonpositive health) and `ValueOverflow` for each base `Int` stat in
     authored order, and `MissingStat` for an absent base `Fixed` stat.
   - **R5** `TooManyTags` if the distinct prefab tags exceed `MAX_TAGS`.

   An empty `ruleset.pools` panics with start's text, `ruleset pools are
   validated nonempty by data read-time checks`.
4. **B4 (registration publication) [a].** On success it calls
   `world.spawn(EntityMeta {})`, which enqueues one `Spawned`. It inserts
   a `CreatureState` with these values:
   - `placement = spec.placement.id` and `ruleset = spec.ruleset.id`;
   - `attributes`, `fixed` and `tags` from `read_creature_stats`;
   - `health = max_health` = the health stat's value;
   - one pool per `ruleset.pools` entry, in order:
     `ResourcePool::new(ResourcePoolId(id), max, max,
     convert_refresh(refresh))`;
   - `suspended` empty.

   It returns the id. It draws no RNG, interns nothing, adds no transform
   and no timeline entry. It is allowed while an encounter is active (T12).
5. **B5 (D6, time outside) [a].** Outside an encounter nothing changes a
   `CreatureState`: `tick`, `request_mode` and every combat operation on
   other entities leave it byte-identical. No `OnTick`, `OnTurnStart`,
   `OnRoundStart` or `OnRest` refresh is delivered, and no suspended
   attachment counts down.
6. **B6 (D7, stats) [a].** `attributes` holds the base non-health,
   non-derived `Int` stats in authored stat order, which is the
   definition's `attribute_names` rule (`combat.rs:1316-1325`). `fixed`
   holds the base non-derived `Fixed` stats in authored order, with the
   prefab's exact raw value. No `Bool`, `Enum`, `Tags`, `Dice` or derived
   stat is stored.
7. **B7 (despawn) [a].** `despawn` strips the `CreatureState` along with
   the rest of the entity and enqueues exactly one `Despawned` (AGENTS
   invariant 1).
8. **B8 (transfer) [a].** `transfer_entity` and `transfer_history_entity`
   carry the `CreatureState` to the new destination id, byte-equal. The
   source store loses it. Preflight and its order are unchanged. An entity
   without a `CreatureState` gets none at the destination.
9. **B9 (load) [a].** `World::deserialize` checks the following, after the
   dangling-timeline loop (`world.rs:145-151`) and before `match
   &repr.combat`, in store order and in this order:
   - **K1** `dangling creature for non-live {id:?}`, as a separate loop
     over all entries first;
   - **K2** `creature state for a combatant`, as a second loop;
   - then, per creature:
     - **K3** `creature with a nonpositive maximum`;
     - **K4** `creature health exceeds its maximum`;
     - **K5** `creature declares no pools`;
     - **K6** `creature declares a duplicate pool`;
     - **K7** `creature stats repeat a name`;
     - **K8** `creature attachments break ascending order` (not strictly
       ascending);
     - **K9** `creature attachment carries no remaining rounds`;
     - **K10** `creature tags are not ascending and distinct`;
     - **K11** `creature tags exceed their bound`.

   A `ResourcePool` with `current > max` already fails in the pool's own
   decoder (`resource.rs:282-292`).
10. **B10 (history) [a].** `HistoryWorld::register_creature` stages the
    registration and journals exactly `[Spawned { entity }]`. Its first
    successful commit upgrades version 1 to 2, for good (D11). A rejected
    registration commits nothing and upgrades nothing.
    `spawn_with_creature` journals one `Spawned`, and its staged world
    upgrades the destination the same way.

### T139b — entering and leaving
11. **B11 (seed: `CreatureState` → `Combatant`) [b].** For an entrant at
    the encounter's current `round`, the seeded `Combatant` has:
    - `placement`, `attributes`, `health` and `max_health` from the
      creature, and `dead = false`;
    - `action_pool = pools[0]` and `extra_pools = pools[1..]`, cloned with
      their current balances. No refresh is applied;
    - `attached`: for each suspended entry in order whose `effect` appears
      in `definition.effects`, an `AttachedEffect { effect, expires_round:
      round.saturating_add(remaining_rounds) }`. Other entries are dropped;
    - `order = None` and `tags = creature.tags`;
    - `persistent = Some(PersistentCarry { fixed: creature.fixed })`;
    - `initiative` per B17.

    The `CreatureState` is removed in the same operation.
12. **B12 (write-back: `Combatant` → `CreatureState`) [b].** A persistent
    combatant leaving at round `r` (captured before any removal or
    advance) becomes a `CreatureState` with:
    - `placement`, `attributes`, `health` and `max_health` from the
      combatant;
    - `ruleset = definition.ruleset` and `fixed` from the carry;
    - `pools = [action_pool] ++ extra_pools`;
    - `suspended`: for each attachment with `expires_round > r`, in order,
      `{effect, remaining_rounds: expires_round - r}`;
    - `tags` from the combatant.

    A dead persistent combatant is written back with `health == 0`. A
    non-persistent combatant writes nothing back (D2).
13. **B13 (member checks) [b].** `start_encounter_with_members` runs every
    existing start check in today's order. After the `CombatDefinition` is
    built (`combat.rs:1878-1899`) and before the initiative draws
    (`:1901`), it checks the members:
    - **M0** `TooManyParticipants` if `participants.len() +
      members.len() > MAX_COMBATANTS`.
    - Then, per member in slice order:
      - **M1** `AbsentActor { actor: entity }` if the entity is not live.
      - **M2** `AlreadyParticipant { entity }` if it appears earlier in
        `members`.
      - **M3** `NotCreature { entity }` if it holds no `CreatureState`.
      - **M4** `DeadActor { actor: entity }` if `creature.health == 0`.
      - **M5** `RulesetMismatch { entity }` in any of these cases:
        `creature.ruleset != definition.ruleset`; the attribute names in
        order differ from `definition.attribute_names`; or `pools` differs
        from `definition.pools` in length, or in any `(id, max, refresh)`
        position.
      - **M6** `PlacementInUse { placement }` if `creature.placement`
        equals an authored participant's resolved placement id, or an
        earlier member's.
      - **M7** `InvalidStatValue { stat }` if a derived stat fails start's
        derived preflight (`:1457-1491`), evaluated with the creature's
        `health` and `attributes`.
      - **M8** `TooManyTags` if `|creature.tags ∪ every grant of
        definition.effects| > MAX_TAGS`.

    An existing check always wins over a member check. Any failure leaves
    the world unchanged: no entity, event, RNG draw, interner entry or
    creature removal.
14. **B14 (member publication) [b].** Initiative draws (T137 B17) run for
    the authored participants first, unchanged, then for each member in
    slice order, by B17's rule. The authored spawn loop runs unchanged.
    Then each member, in slice order, has its creature removed and its
    seeded `Combatant` inserted (B11, round 0). It is then keyed at
    `InitiativeKey(initiative)` in TB or `InitiativeKey(0)` in RT. Members
    are not spawned and enqueue no `Spawned`. Every step from the
    roll-tag intern onward is unchanged (`:1992-2014`), so the TB head and
    its `TurnStart` refresh may fall on a member. `members == &[]` is
    byte-identical to `start_encounter_in_mode`, including RNG and events.
15. **B15 (join checks) [b].** `join_encounter` checks in this order:
    - **J1** `NoEncounter`.
    - **J2** `EncounterTerminal` if `active == None` in TB, or if fewer
      than two combatants are alive in RT (D5).
    - **J3** `TooManyParticipants` if `combatants.len() + 1 >
      MAX_COMBATANTS`.
    - **J4** `AbsentActor { actor: entity }` if the entity is not live.
    - **J5** `AlreadyParticipant { entity }` if it holds a `Combatant`.
    - **J6** `NotCreature`.
    - **J7** `DeadActor`.
    - **J8** `RulesetMismatch`, as M5.
    - **J9** `PlacementInUse` if a current combatant, dead or alive, has
      the creature's placement.
    - **J10** `InvalidStatValue`, as M7; then `TooManyTags`, as M8.

    Any failure leaves the world unchanged: bytes, RNG, events and
    interners.
16. **B16 (join publication) [b].** After B17's key, the creature is
    removed and the seeded `Combatant` is inserted (B11, current `round`).
    Then B18 or B19 schedules it. No pool refreshes on entry (T10: entry is
    not a turn or round start). No `SimEvent` is enqueued, `active` is not
    changed, and the world interner gains nothing (T137 D6).
17. **B17 (entry initiative) [b].** If `definition.initiative` is
    `Some(rule)`, the key is computed as at start (`combat.rs:1903-1943`):
    - on a scratch clone of the world interner, `bonus` = the saturating
      sum of `query_int` over `rule.bonus_stats`, using the entrant's
      `health` and `attributes`, no tags and no modifiers;
    - then one `rule.dice.evaluate(rng, COMBAT_ROLL_STREAM)`;
    - `initiative = rolled.total.saturating_add(bonus).saturating_add(entrant.initiative)`.

    Otherwise `initiative = entrant.initiative` and nothing is drawn. This
    holds in both modes: an RT joiner stores the key for a later RT→TB
    rebuild.
18. **B18 (D4, TB join) [b].**
    - If `round == 0` and `(InitiativeKey(initiative), entity)` sorts after
      the head entry, the joiner is inserted at `InitiativeKey(initiative)`
      at once.
    - Otherwise it gets no timeline entry. It is an alive combatant (a
      valid target, counted by every alive count) and enters at the next
      rollover at its key, with that rollover's `RoundStart` refresh.

    A waiting joiner keeps the encounter from going terminal: a timeline
    that empties with it and one other alive combatant rolls the round
    over.
19. **B19 (RT join) [b].** The joiner is inserted at
    `InitiativeKey(elapsed as i32)` (`elapsed <= RT_CLOCK_MAX`, so the
    cast is exact), with `order: None`. It is due at once and acts on the
    first tick after it receives an order (T138 B17).
20. **B20 (release) [b].** `end_encounter` builds the summary as today.
    Then, in combatant store order, it removes each timeline entry and
    combatant, and inserts the B12 `CreatureState` for each persistent
    one, using `definition.ruleset` and `round` captured before removal.
    It emits no event. The summary still lists every combatant, members
    included.
21. **B21 (leave checks) [b].** `leave_encounter` uses `validate_action`'s
    actor order (`combat.rs:2603-2617`):
    - **V1** `NoEncounter`.
    - **V2** `NotParticipant { entity }` if the entity is live but not a
      combatant.
    - **V3** `AbsentActor { actor: entity }` if it is not live.
    - **V4** `DeadActor { actor: entity }` if the combatant is dead. The
      dead await release or despawn.

    Any failure leaves the world unchanged.
22. **B22 (leave settlement) [b].** Capture `round` and
    `definition.ruleset`. Then call `remove_combatant_tracked(entity)`,
    which removes the combatant and its timeline entry and settles the
    encounter as `despawn` does:
    - if the active combatant left, the encounter advances (new head plus
      `TurnStart`, rollover, a pending switch, or terminal);
    - if any other combatant left, the turn is untouched;
    - if the last combatant left, `combat = None`, and interners, events,
      RNG and tick are retained;
    - in RT, dropping the alive count from two or more to below two ends
      the encounter.

    Then, if the combatant was persistent, insert its B12 `CreatureState`.
    The entity stays live, keeping its `Transform`. Others' standing orders
    that target it fail when due (`OrderEnded`, T138 B34). It returns the
    helper's facts.
23. **B23 (leave vs death, despawn and release) [b].**

    | | Death | Leave | Despawn | Release |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | Entity live after | yes | yes | no | yes |
    | `Combatant` kept | yes (dead) | no | no | no |
    | Timeline entry | removed | removed | removed | removed |
    | Becomes `CreatureState` | no | iff persistent | no | iff persistent |
    | `SimEvent` | `Died` | none | `Despawned` | none |
    | Settles turn / releases on last | via the action | yes | yes | releases all |
24. **B24 (despawn unchanged) [b].** After the D10 extraction, `despawn`
    and `despawn_tracked` give the same bytes, events and facts as today
    for every input.
25. **B25 (load) [b].** Inside the per-combatant loop (`world.rs:476-588`),
    after the placement check, add **P1** `combatant persistent stats
    repeat a name`, for a carry whose `fixed` names repeat. A save holding
    a persistent combatant round-trips.
26. **B26 (determinism) [b].** Every new operation is all-or-nothing. Two
    runs of one script, with the same seed and the same registrations,
    starts, joins, leaves, actions, ticks and release, give equal
    per-step `state_hash` sequences in one binary. A run saved and
    reloaded mid-encounter, while a TB joiner is waiting, continues with
    equal hashes.

### T139c — the journal
27. **B27 (payloads) [c].** `joined` and `left` use the adjacent-tag
    encodings in "Hash and history", with `type` before `value`. A
    duplicate, unknown or missing `entity` key fails with the existing
    `EntityRef` messages, and `value` before `type` fails as today.
28. **B28 (emission order) [c].** Each wrapper method stages a clone, runs
    the free function, drains the legacy queue exactly once, and commits.
    - `start_encounter_with_members` journals `Spawned` per authored
      participant (drain order), then `Joined` per member (slice order),
      then `TurnStarted` if the start scheduled a head.
    - `join_encounter` journals `[Joined]`.
    - `leave_encounter` journals `Left`, then `push_transition(facts)`:
      `ModeChanged` and `RoundStarted` if a pending switch applied, then
      `TurnStarted`, or else `EncounterEnded`, as the facts say. Leaving
      the last combatant of a nonterminal TB encounter journals
      `EncounterEnded`; of a terminal one, nothing more.
    - `end_encounter` still journals nothing.

    Gameplay errors win before history errors (`HistoryError::Combat`). A
    rejected operation commits nothing, including the RNG draws of a
    rolled join.
29. **B29 (version) [c].** `HISTORY_VERSION` stays 2. A wrapper upgrades
    1 → 2 at the first commit whose staged world holds an RT-capable
    encounter, a `CreatureState` or a persistent combatant, or whose
    events include a v2 variant (now five). It never goes back.
30. **B30 (replay) [c].** Two `HistoryWorld` runs of one script give equal
    per-step `history_hash` sequences. A wrapper saved and reloaded
    mid-script continues with equal hashes.

## Errors
`CombatError` gains exactly the five T139b variants. Existing variants
reused, with their existing meanings: `NoEncounter`, `AbsentActor`,
`DeadActor`, `NotParticipant`, `TooManyParticipants`, `TooManyStats`,
`TooManyTags`, `AreaMismatch`, `MissingCreature`, `MissingStat`,
`InvalidStatValue` and `ValueOverflow`. The new precedence lists are R1–R5
(B3), M0–M8 (B13), J1–J10 (B15) and V1–V4 (B21). No existing path's
precedence moves: member checks follow every start check, and an empty
`members` runs none. `HistoryError` and `AreaError` are unchanged. No
crate outside `crpg-sim` matches `CombatError` exhaustively
(`crpg-server/tests/host_capture.rs:852-875` uses `matches!`).

## Open questions for the user
1. **Which combatants come out as `CreatureState`?** *Recommend* (a): only
   persistent ones (those that entered from a `CreatureState`), at both
   leave and release (D2). No golden moves. (b) Every live combatant at
   release and leave. This is simpler to explain, but it moves
   `history_v1.*` and `surface_v1.*` (four files), needs their
   re-baseline, and makes every authored monster persistent.
2. **Persistent members at start.** *Recommend* (a):
   `start_encounter_with_members` (D3), so a party competes for the first
   turn. (b) Join only. Then a party that starts a fight can enter round
   0 only behind the first head, or only at round 1 under 3(a).
3. **A TB round-0 joiner whose slot has passed.** *Recommend* (a): it
   waits for the rollover. This is T14's "never ahead of the active" read
   literally, needs no new state and matches the later-round rule (D4).
   (b) It acts right after the current head. This needs a
   `CombatState.pending_joins` field and a change to `advance_tracked`.
4. **Join into a terminal encounter.** *Recommend* (a): reject with
   `EncounterTerminal` (D5). (b) Revive an RT encounter that reaches two
   alive. It would journal a second `EncounterEnded`, and it re-opens
   T138 B18.
5. **Time outside encounters.** *Recommend* (a): nothing refreshes or
   expires. Effects are suspended with their remaining rounds and resume
   at entry (D6). (b) Strip effects at release and at leave. (c) Strip
   at release, suspend at leave.
6. **Non-`Int` persistent stats.** *Recommend* (a): store base `Fixed`
   values only, because data supplies no other per-creature value (D7).
   (b) Add `Bool`/`Enum`/`Tags`/`Dice` defaults, which needs a
   `crpg-data` schema change and migration (S-set) first.
7. **History version.** *Recommend* (a): stay at 2 and widen the v2
   trigger (D11). No golden or existing test moves. (b) Bump to 3,
   lazily. That needs your approval to edit six assertions:
   `tests/history.rs:832`, `tests/history_v2.rs:623`, `:764` and `:877`,
   and `tests/history_v2_contract.rs:468` and `:524`.
8. **A join/leave golden.** *Recommend* (a): none now; add it with the RT
   host in T141. (b) A `history_join` golden per target now, which needs
   H-golden approval.
9. **Split.** *Recommend* (a): T139a → T139b → T139c, plus companions
   T139d and T139e. (b) Muse's two packages (store and lifecycle, then
   join, leave and journal). That review is about 1 900 lines, and it
   pairs the despawn refactor with the payloads.
