## Task
ADR-0036 U2–U7 (`:93–130`), R1–R5 (`:134–163`), M1–M7 (`:167–222`), W1–W7
(`:226–273`), C2–C5 (`:286–300`) and S2 (`:317–325`), plus the `snap`
export S3 names (`:327–333`): authoritative movement in `crpg-sim`.
Positions are `[Fx16_16; 3]`; `Transform` becomes an `f32` mirror the sim
writes. The host binds a navmesh revision and hands sim whole routes it
validated nothing about except what sim can check without geometry. Stage 4
walks routes every tick at each mover's speed. A turn-based participant
moves only by one atomic, pool-paid `perform_move`, journaled as history
version 3. Sim never imports `crpg-nav` (A2, `:68–72`).

- **T036a — state and operations.** Pure length, step and projection
  functions; `MoveRoute`, `NavRevision`, `RouteTag`, `Measure`; `Mover` and
  `World.movers`/`World.nav`; mover creation at registration and start;
  `bind_nav`, `place`, `set_route`, `clear_route` with history wrappers;
  despawn and transfer; load coherence. No tick change.
- **T036b — stage 4.** `movement_system` after stage 3, the movement facts
  and their public reader, the three transitions that stop movers (W3), and
  the `movement_v1` golden.
- **T036c — the turn-based move.** `TurnMovement` on the combat
  definition, `route_cost`, `validate_move`/`perform_move`,
  `HistoryEvent::Moved`, history version 3, and the `history_v3` golden.

## Readings of the ADR
Where ADR-0036 leaves a detail open or a code fact forces a choice, this
contract decides it. Each needs the user's approval with the contract.
1. **Three parts, not two.** The ADR offers a two-way split (`:539`). T036a
   alone is about 700 source lines; stage 4 and its golden are separable and
   testable on their own, so they are T036b.
2. **Step is stored on the mover (Question 2).** M1's `Mover` gains
   `step: Fx16_16`, resolved at creation with `speed` from the same
   `LengthWire` rules. C2 says step is "resolved at action time"
   (`:288–289`); in v1 no operation changes a `Fixed` stat (effects target
   `Int` stats only, and `Combatant.fixed` and `CreatureState.fixed` are
   never written after creation), so the stored value is the value an
   action-time read returns. A derived `Fixed` length would otherwise need
   its formula persisted in the combat definition. `InvalidStep` therefore
   becomes a creation error (`CombatError::InvalidStep`), and the load check
   L3 rejects a non-positive stored step.
3. **Derived `Fixed` lengths are evaluated** (T148 Reading 5: "T036 resolves
   it"), once, at creation, by the rules kernel over the creature's base
   `Fixed` values (B-L2).
4. **Creation errors are `CombatError` variants** (`InvalidPosition`,
   `InvalidSpeed`, `InvalidStep`), because `register_creature` and start
   return `CombatError`. They run after every existing check, so no existing
   precedence changes: registration after R6, start after `TooManyTags` and
   before S1 (`crates/crpg-sim/AGENTS.md:574`, "add a new start check before
   S1-S4").
5. **Only a spawned participant gets a new mover.** Enter participants,
   members and joiners already hold the mover their registration created
   (the encounter's ruleset is checked equal, S1/M5/J8), and keep its
   position. Join never spawns, so M2's "join" (`:182`) creates nothing.
6. **"Dead" means a dead combatant or a dead record (Question 3).** M4's
   `DeadCombatant` and W1's `Cancelled { Dead }` also apply to a creature
   record with `health == 0`, so a written-back corpse cannot walk.
7. **The movement facts get a public reader (Question 4).** W1 puts
   `Arrived`/`Cancelled` in `TickFacts` "for the host" (`:242–243`), but
   `TickFacts` is crate-private (`crates/crpg-sim/src/combat.rs:1235`).
   `tick_with_movement` (free and `HistoryWorld`) returns them; `tick`
   delegates and discards them.
8. **W4's turn checks are exactly `validate_action(EndTurn { actor })`**:
   `NoEncounter`, `NotParticipant`, `AbsentActor`, `DeadActor`, then
   `WrongMode { RealTime }` before `OutOfTurn`, wrapped as
   `MoveError::Combat`. One legality source (`crates/crpg-sim/AGENTS.md:268`).
9. **`CannotAfford { cost: u64, current: u32 }`.** The cost is computed in
   `u64`, so a cost above `u32::MAX` is reported as its true value.
10. **`NotMover` also covers a turn-based encounter whose definition holds no
    movement rules** (only reachable from a hand-edited save).
11. **Mirror rewrites.** Creation, `place`, `set_route`, `clear_route`,
    `perform_move` and transfer write the mover's mirror with zero velocity.
    `bind_nav` and the W3 transitions rewrite only the movers whose route
    they cleared. Stage 4 rewrites every mover every tick (M3, `:188–191`).
12. **Load coherence** is the twelve ordered rules L1–L12 (B-L6), a superset
    of M7's list (`:218–222`) that also bounds speed, step and position.
    Comparing the `f32` mirror with `to_f32(position)` is validation, not a
    rules read; nothing converts `f32` back (U2).
13. **History version 3** follows the D16 pattern: a new wrapper still starts
    at 1; the first committed `Moved` sets 3; 1 and 2 stay readable; a
    wrapper below 3 holding a `Moved` fails to load. `HISTORY_VERSION`
    becomes 3, which forces seven mechanical edits to four existing tests
    (Question 5).
14. **`seg_len` and `point_at` are total** (no panic for any input):
    `seg_len` squares in `u128`, `point_at` multiplies in `i128`. Within U3
    they equal U4's `u64` and U5's `i64` formulas exactly (as T035 Reading
    10 does in nav).
15. **`snap` (S3) is exported here** as the identity, because only
    `crpg-sim` can hold it and T037 calls it.
16. **`NavRevision` serializes as derived serde** (an array of 32 numbers).
17. **The start checks the movement pool** (T036c): a pool outside the
    ruleset's templates is `MissingPool`, a zero step cost is `InvalidCost`,
    after the T036a movement checks and before S1. Data rules both out
    (T148 B5, B6).

## Shared definitions
- `p(x, z)` is `[Fx16_16::from_int(x), Fx16_16::ZERO, Fx16_16::from_int(z)]`;
  `rp(x, y, z)` is the point with those raw values. `m = 65536`.
- **inside(v)**: `raw(v).unsigned_abs() <= COORD_LIMIT_RAW as u32` (never
  `abs()`, which overflows on `i32::MIN`).
- **dead(world, e)**: `world.combatants().get(e)` is `Some(c)` with
  `c.dead()`, or `world.creatures().get(e)` is `Some(r)` with `r.dead()`.
- **mirror(mover, velocity)**: `Transform { position: [to_f32(x), to_f32(y),
  to_f32(z)] of mover.position, velocity }`, inserted into `transforms`
  under the mover's entity (replacing). "Zero velocity" is `[0.0; 3]`.
- **MOVE fixture** (tests and schedules). Start from
  `support::Fixture::standard()` (`crates/crpg-sim/tests/support/mod.rs:79`)
  and edit its documents through JSON (serialize, edit, deserialize):
  ruleset `stats` gains `{"name":"pace","kind":"fixed"}`; `pools` gains
  `{"id":uid(106),"max":6,"refresh":{"type":"on_turn_start"}}`; `movement`
  is `{"speed":{"type":"stat","value":"pace"},"turn":{"pool":uid(106),
  "step":{"type":"constant","value":98304},"step_cost":1}}`. Creature 121
  (A) gains `pace` raw `65536`, creature 122 (B) `pace` raw `32768`.

## Behaviour

### T036a: arithmetic (U4–U6, S2, S3)
1. **B-U1 (U4).** `ceil_sqrt(n: u128) = s + (s * s < n) as u128` with
   `s = n.isqrt()` (crate-private). `seg_len(a, b)` is
   `ceil_sqrt(dx * dx + dz * dz)` with `dx`, `dz` the raw differences as
   `u128` magnitudes (`(i64::from(b) - i64::from(a)).unsigned_abs()`),
   returned as `u64` (always fits). `y` is ignored.
2. **B-U2 (U5).** `point_at(a, b, len, d)`: if `len == 0` it is `a`; if
   `d >= len` it is `b` exactly; otherwise each of the three components is
   `a + ((b - a) * d) / len` computed in `i128` with Rust's truncating `/`,
   then converted back (it lies between `a` and `b`, so it fits `i32`).
3. **B-U3 (C4, S2).** `route_length(points)` is `None` when
   `points.len() < 2` or any component of any point fails **inside**;
   otherwise the checked `u64` sum of `seg_len` over consecutive pairs
   (`None` on overflow). `route_measure(Measure::Euclidean, points)` equals
   `route_length(points)`.
4. **B-U4 (S3).** `snap(measure, point)` returns `point` unchanged. No sim
   operation calls it.
5. **B-U5 (U6).** `to_f32(v)` is `(raw(v) as f32) / 65536.0`. It is called
   only to write mirrors; no movement or rules code reads an `f32`.

### T036a: routes (R1–R5)
6. **B-R1 (R4).** `validate_route(route, nav, start)` checks, in order, the
   first failure winning: `points.len()` outside `2..=MAX_ROUTE_POINTS` is
   `RouteSize { points }`; the first point (by index) with a component
   failing **inside** (`x`, `y`, `z` in turn) is `OutOfBounds { index }`;
   the first `i` with `seg_len(points[i], points[i + 1]) == 0` is
   `ZeroSegment { index: i }`; a total above `MAX_ROUTE_LENGTH_RAW` is
   `TooLong { length }`; `nav == None` is `NavUnbound`; `nav !=
   Some(route.revision)` is `StaleRevision`; `points[0] != start` in any of
   the three components is `StartMismatch`. Otherwise `Ok(total)`.
7. **B-R2 (R2).** `NavRevision::of_bytes(bytes)` is `NavRevision(*blake3::hash(bytes).as_bytes())`.

### T036a: creation (M2, Readings 2–5)
8. **B-L1.** When the ruleset's `movement` is `Some(m)`, a mover is built
   for one placement and one creature's base `Fixed` values
   (`CreatureStats.fixed`, `crates/crpg-sim/src/combat.rs:1284–1293`), in
   this order: a position component failing **inside** is
   `InvalidPosition { placement }`; `speed = resolve(m.speed)` failing, or
   `raw(speed)` outside `1..=crpg_data::MAX_SPEED_PER_TICK_RAW`, is
   `InvalidSpeed { placement }`; `step = resolve(m.turn.step)` failing, or
   `raw(step) < 1`, is `InvalidStep { placement }`. The mover is
   `{ position: placement.transform.position, speed, step, route: None }`.
   `placement` is the placement's id. Without `movement`, nothing is built
   and nothing is checked.
9. **B-L2 (Reading 3).** `resolve(length)`: `Constant(v)` is `v`.
   `Stat(name)` is the creature's base `Fixed` value of `name` when it has
   one. Otherwise, when the ruleset declares `name` as a derived `Fixed`
   stat, it is evaluated by the kernel: a scratch `Interners::new()`; one
   `StatDefinition { kind: StatKind::Fixed }` per `Fixed` stat of the
   ruleset in authored order (derived ones carry their formula converted
   node for node: a `Fixed` literal becomes `Expr::Literal(StatValue::Fixed)`,
   a `Stat` name of a `Fixed` stat becomes `Expr::Stat`, operators map one to
   one); a `StatBlock` of the base `Fixed` values; `ModifierPipeline::new(
   definitions, BTreeMap::new())`; one `query` with no tags and no
   modifiers on a probe entity, as start's initiative preflight does
   (`crates/crpg-sim/src/combat.rs:2161–2193`). A non-`Fixed` literal or
   name, a pipeline error, a query error (a zero divisor) or a non-`Fixed`
   result fails the resolution. Every other `Stat(name)` fails. Nothing
   panics; nothing is interned into the world.
10. **B-L3 (registration).** `register_creature`
    (`crates/crpg-sim/src/creature.rs:143`) runs B-L1 after R6 (`:186–189`)
    and before any mutation, as R7–R9. On success it inserts, after the
    record, the mover and `mirror(mover, zero)`. Its doc comment drops
    "adds no transform" for movement rulesets.
11. **B-L4 (start).** `start_encounter_with_members_tracked` runs B-L1 for
    every authored participant, in authored order, after the lifecycle is
    built (`crates/crpg-sim/src/combat.rs:2065–2075`) and before the T139 B13
    resolution loop (`:2077`), so every existing check still wins. In the
    publication loop, a `Source::Spawn` participant (`:2254`) gets its mover
    and `mirror(mover, zero)` after its combatant is inserted. Enter
    participants and members get nothing (Reading 5). TB and RT starts
    alike.
12. **B-L5.** Creation draws no RNG, interns nothing in the world and
    enqueues no event beyond today's.

### T036a: operations (M4, all-or-nothing)
13. **B-O1.** `bind_nav(revision)`: `nav() == Some(revision)` changes
    nothing and returns `[]`. Otherwise `nav` becomes `Some(revision)`,
    every mover's route is cleared (position unchanged) and each cleared
    mover's mirror is rewritten with zero velocity; the cleared
    `(entity, tag)` pairs return in ascending `EntityId`.
14. **B-O2.** `place(entity, position)`: not live is
    `AbsentEntity`; no mover is `NotMover`; a component failing **inside**
    is `OutOfBounds { index: 0 }`. Then the position is set, the route is
    cleared and the mirror is written with zero velocity.
15. **B-O3.** `set_route(entity, route)`: `AbsentEntity`; `NotMover`;
    **dead** is `DeadCombatant`; a combatant of an encounter whose mode is
    `TurnBased` is `TurnBased`; then `validate_route(&route, nav(),
    mover.position)`. On success the route becomes
    `ActiveRoute { route, segment: 0, along: 0 }`, the mirror is written with
    zero velocity, and the replaced route's tag (or `None`) returns. A
    combatant of an RT encounter (pending switch or not) and every
    non-participant may set a route.
16. **B-O4.** `clear_route(entity)`: `AbsentEntity`; `NotMover`. Then the
    route is cleared, the mirror is written with zero velocity and the
    cleared tag (or `None`) returns.
17. **B-O5.** A failing operation changes nothing: the world serializes to
    the same bytes. No operation draws RNG, enqueues an event or advances
    time.
18. **B-O6.** Each `HistoryWorld` wrapper stages a clone, runs the `World`
    operation, maps its error to `HistoryError::Move`, and commits zero
    events (so it needs no free journal slot or sequence and never changes
    `version`).

### T036a: lifecycle (M6) and identity
19. **B-M1.** `despawn` removes the mover with the entity
    (`World::despawn_tracked`, `crates/crpg-sim/src/world.rs:990–1000`).
20. **B-M2.** `transfer_entity` and `transfer_history_entity`
    (`crates/crpg-sim/src/area.rs:158–220`) copy the source mover with

21. **B-M3.** `leave_encounter` and `end_encounter` leave movers alone.
22. **B-M4 (M1).** A world with no mover and no bound revision serializes
    byte-identically to today: neither `movers` nor `nav` is written. When
    present they are the last two top-level keys, `movers` first.

### T036a: load coherence (M7, Reading 12)
23. **B-L6.** `World::deserialize` checks every mover in store order, after
    the creature checks (`crates/crpg-sim/src/world.rs:160–244`) and before

    - L6 a transform position component not `==` the `to_f32` of the

    - then, when it has a route: L7 `nav` is `None`: `mover route without a bound navmesh`;
      L8 `nav != Some(route.revision)`: `mover route revision differs from the bound navmesh`;

      L10 `segment as usize >= points.len() - 1`: `mover route segment out of range`;
      L11 `along >= seg_len(points[segment], points[segment + 1])`: `mover route progress out of range`;
      L12 `position != point_at(points[segment], points[segment + 1], that seg_len, along)`:

### T036b: stage 4 (W1, W5, W6, C5)
24. **B-W1.** `run_systems` (`crates/crpg-sim/src/tick.rs:53–59`) appends
    `movement_system(world, facts)` after `timeline_system`, commented
    `// §10 stage 4 (movement) — T036: routes advance, mirrors rewrite.` It
    runs on every tick, with or without an encounter, in both modes and
    after an RT clock freeze.
25. **B-W2.** For each mover holding a route, in ascending `EntityId`:
    ```text
    old = position
    if dead(world, entity): clear route; push Cancelled{entity, tag, Dead}; displacement = 0; next
    budget = raw(speed) as u64
    loop:
      len  = seg_len(p[segment], p[segment+1]); left = len - along
      if budget < left: along += budget; position = point_at(p[segment], p[segment+1], len, along); break
      budget -= left; segment += 1; along = 0
      if segment == p.len() - 1: position = p[last]; clear route; push Arrived{entity, tag}; break
    displacement = position - old   (per component, raw i32)
    ```
    Leftover budget at arrival is discarded. Then every mover (routed or
    not) gets `mirror(mover, [to_f32(displacement)…])`, a mover with no
    route this tick getting zero velocity. A missing transform is
    re-inserted.
26. **B-W3.** Stage 4 never touches a pool, an order, the timeline, the RNG
    or the event queue (C5, W6). An RT order does not clear a route and a
    route does not clear an order.
27. **B-W4.** `tick(world)` is `let _ = tick_with_movement(world);`, and
    `tick_with_movement` returns `tick_tracked(world).moves`.
    `HistoryWorld::tick` becomes `self.tick_with_movement().map(|_| ())`;
    the journal is exactly what it was (movement facts are not history
    events).

### T036b: transitions stop movers (W2, W3)
28. **B-T1.** A crate-private `stop(world, entity) -> Option<RouteTag>`:
    when the entity's mover holds a route, it clears it, rewrites the
    mirror with zero velocity and returns the tag; otherwise (no mover, or
    no route) it does nothing and returns `None`. It is the one way W3
    clears routes.
29. **B-T2.** A turn-based start (`start_encounter_with_members_tracked` with

    stops nobody.
30. **B-T3.** `join_encounter` (`crates/crpg-sim/src/combat.rs:2580`) into a
    turn-based encounter calls `stop` on the joiner after it becomes a
    combatant. An RT join stops nobody.
31. **B-T4.** `switch_to_turn_based` (`:4296`) calls `stop` on every
    combatant, dead or alive, before `tb_round_start`. So on the switching
    tick stage 2 clears the routes and stage 4 moves nobody of the
    encounter. TB-to-RT, `leave_encounter` and `end_encounter` stop nobody.

### T036c: the turn-based move (W4, C2–C4)
32. **B-C1 (C4).** `route_cost(measured, step, step_cost)`: `raw(step) <= 0`
    is `None`; otherwise `measured.div_ceil(raw(step) as u64)` times
    `step_cost` in `u64` (`None` on overflow), then `u32::try_from(..).ok()`.
33. **B-C2 (data).** Start sets `CombatDefinition.movement` from
    `ruleset.movement`: `measure` (absent is `Euclidean`), `turn.pool`,
    `turn.step_cost`; `None` without `movement`. After the T036a movement
    checks and before S1: a pool not among the ruleset's pool templates is
    `MissingPool { pool }`; `step_cost == 0` is
    `InvalidCost { cost: 0, max: <that pool's max> }`.
34. **B-C3.** `validate_move(world, actor, route)`, read-only, checks in
    order: `validate_action(world, &CombatAction::EndTurn { actor })`, its
    error wrapped as `Combat(..)` (Reading 8); no mover for `actor`, or the
    definition's `movement` is `None`, is `NotMover { entity: actor }`;
    `validate_route(route, nav(), mover.position)`; then
    `measured = route_measure(movement.measure, &route.points)`,
    `cost = measured.div_ceil(raw(mover.step)) * step_cost` in `u64`, and
    `current` the actor's balance of `movement.pool`; `cost > current` is
    `CannotAfford { cost, current }`. Otherwise `Ok(cost as u32)`.
35. **B-C4.** `perform_move` runs `validate_move` first and then, with no
    other check: spends exactly `cost` from that pool
    (`ResourcePool::spend`); sets the mover's position to the last point;
    clears its route; writes `mirror(mover, zero)`. It keeps the turn,
    draws no RNG, enqueues no event, never ends the turn, and may repeat
    while the pool pays (C3). It returns
    `MoveOutcome { actor, cost, from: <old position>, to: <last point> }`.
    A failure changes nothing.
36. **B-C5.** `HistoryWorld::perform_move` stages a clone, runs
    `perform_move` (error: `HistoryError::Move`), drains the (empty) legacy
    queue and commits exactly `[Moved { entity: actor, points:
    route.points.clone() }]`.

## Questions for the user
1. **Split T036 into three parts instead of two?** The engine change is big
   (about 1,200 lines of code plus tests). The ADR suggested two parts.
   Three parts (a: positions, routes and the host's operations, no tick
   change; b: walking every tick and the "stop moving when a turn-based
   fight starts" rule; c: paid turn-based moves and the history version)
   keep each review small, and each part is testable alone. Two parts mean
   fewer reviews but one very large first part. *Recommend: three.*
2. **Store the turn-based step length on the creature when it is created?**
   The ruleset says how long one paid "step" is (a constant or a creature
   stat). The ADR said to read it at move time; storing it at creation gives
   the same number today, because nothing can change those stats yet, and it
   avoids saving the stat formulas inside every fight. The cost: when a
   later feature lets effects change such stats, that task must refresh the
   stored value. *Recommend: store it.*
3. **Treat a dead creature outside a fight as dead for movement?** The ADR
   only stops dead *combatants* from walking. A creature that died in a
   fight is written back as a dead record when the fight is released;
   without this rule the host could still make the corpse walk.
   *Recommend: yes, dead records cannot get a route and lose any route.*
4. **Add a public `tick_with_movement` that reports arrivals?** The host
   needs "this creature arrived" and "this route was dropped because it
   died" to tell clients, but the ADR put those facts in an internal
   structure. The new function ticks exactly as `tick` does and returns
   them; nothing else changes. *Recommend: yes.*
5. **Approve seven one-line edits to four existing history tests.** Four
   tests pin "the newest history version is 2" or use 3 as the example of
   an unknown version. Version 3 now exists, so they change to 3 and 4
   (listed under "Existing-test edits"); nothing they prove is weakened.
   *Recommend: approve.*
6. **Approve two new pairs of golden files (Windows and Linux each):**
   `movement_v1` (a real-time walk with a turn-based fight starting while
   another creature keeps walking) and `history_v3` (paid turn-based moves).
   No existing golden changes. You approve the generated files, with their
   hashes, when T036b and T036c finish. *Recommend: approve the plan.*
7. **Approve the two one-line companion edits** in `crpg-server` and a
   `crpg-net` test, landing with T036c, so the workspace builds when the
   new history event appears. The server does not show turn-based moves to
   clients until T026p. *Recommend: approve.*
