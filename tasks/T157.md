## Task

The user wants a playable slice soon (brief, 2026-11-22): walk around in real
time, enter a fight at an enemy, move the active hero by clicking the ground
with a per-turn movement allowance and attack by clicking the enemy, then walk
again when the fight ends, all in one Godot scene, with the enemy played by
the same server AI as the dedicated server, running in the background host.

What exists, found in code (items 3–6 correct the fact sheet, the draft or
T156's forward note):

1. **There is no exploration without an encounter in a bridge session.**
   Every open starts the authored encounter in a host-chosen mode before the
   host exists (`crates/crpg-godot/src/core/mod.rs:769–772`), and `Host` has
   no start, end, join or leave operation (its public list,
   `crates/crpg-server/src/host.rs:979–5494`). Today's walk scene "walks"
   inside a real-time encounter (`apps/client/scripts/Walk.gd:32`,
   `open_v4_rt`).
2. **Real time to turn-based is an existing, host-authorised switch.** A
   `RequestMode` intent needs the session's `pause` right
   (`crates/crpg-server/src/host.rs:6175–6176`); the sim sets `pending_mode`
   (`crates/crpg-sim/src/combat.rs:4366–4384`) and applies it at the next
   real-time round boundary, stopping every route and starting the
   turn-based round (`combat.rs:4469–4486`, `:4526–4537`). The bridge binds
   every player with `PauseRights::default()` (both false,
   `crates/crpg-godot/src/core/mod.rs:618`), so today a request would be
   refused `NotAuthorized` (17). The draft's "the client has pause rights in
   single-player" is wrong.
3. **A fight ends only when fewer than two combatants are alive.** The sim
   has no sides: `alive < 2` makes the encounter terminal
   (`crates/crpg-sim/src/combat.rs:3885–3898`). With the walk campaign's two
   heroes, killing the goblin does not end the fight.
4. **A finished turn-based fight freezes its survivors.** `set_route`
   refuses every participant of a turn-based encounter
   (`crates/crpg-sim/src/movement.rs:784–791`), terminal or not, and two
   tests pin that for a terminal one (`crates/crpg-sim/tests/movement_core.rs:928–931`,
   `crates/crpg-sim/tests/movement_core_contract.rs:1194–1198`); the host's
   lane-1 window refuses them `WrongMode` first
   (`crates/crpg-server/src/host.rs:4061–4068`). A pending switch back to
   real time "never" applies in a terminal encounter (`combat.rs:4372–4373`).
   The draft's "the server's `ModeChanged` flips the view back to RT" cannot
   happen.
5. **The embedded host does bind a navigation mesh.** `bind_area_bounds`
   bakes the area box and calls `bind_nav_mesh`, which stores it
   (`crates/crpg-server/src/host.rs:4605`, `:4618–4628`), and `drive_ai`
   hands that mesh to `decide` (`crates/crpg-server/src/host/ai.rs:213`,
   `:249`). T156's note (and the draft) that the bridge binds "no navigation
   mesh" is wrong; `combat_basic`'s goblin never walks because its ruleset
   declares no `movement`, so it has no mover (`crates/crpg-ai/src/mvp.rs:162–164`).
6. **The walk campaign cannot show a movement allowance.** Its `movement`
   pool is the action pool `P` (max 1) that Strike also spends, its step is
   1 m, and Strike (ability/1) has no `range`, so in a turn a hero can move
   1 m or attack from anywhere (`campaigns/demo/walk/rulesets/minimal-d6.json`,
   `campaigns/demo/walk/abilities/strike.json`,
   `crates/crpg-data/src/combat.rs:693–695`).
7. **The server AI already plays turn-based turns and waits in real time.**
   `ServerAiHook` gates on "the NPC peer has legal options"
   (`crates/crpg-godot/src/core/authority/v4.rs:203`), and legal options
   exist only for a turn head (`crates/crpg-server/src/host.rs:1107–1117`;
   in real time `active` is `None`). On a ruleset with movement it walks the
   enemy up to its pool and strikes in reach (`crates/crpg-ai/src/mvp.rs:139–231`).
8. **Everything the scene needs on the view already exists.** `active()` is
   the turn head in turn-based mode and 0 in real time and after the end
   (`crates/crpg-godot/src/core/client_v4.rs:475–480`, test
   `crates/crpg-godot/src/core/authority/v4/tests/rt.rs:192`); `declare_move`
   moves the current options' actor (`client_v4.rs:231–254`); move refusals
   reach the HUD log as receipts (`apps/client/scripts/CombatHud.gd:147–149`,
   `:203–209`).

## Readings
Design decisions this contract makes. Each needs approval with the contract.

1. **Walking is the encounter's real-time mode.** Fact 1 leaves no other
   place without host encounter operations: the session opens the area's
   authored encounter in real time; nobody acts in real time (Reading 2), so
   it plays as exploration. ADR-0029's "outside an encounter every entity is
   in real time" is matched in behaviour, not in state. Exploration with no
   encounter, and starting or releasing encounters mid-session, are follow-up
   F-lifecycle.
2. **The server AI plays only turn-based turns.** The walk open reuses
   `ServerAiHook` unchanged (fact 7), so the enemy waits while you walk and
   plays its turns once the fight starts. This is deliberately not T156's
   "real-time AI open" (an enemy that fights in real time), which still needs
   its own gate as T156 says (F-rt-ai). The open is named `open_v4_walk`, not
   `open_v4_rt_ai`, so the name does not promise a real-time AI.
3. **The fight starts by the player's command (D79a).** Clicking a
   non-player proxy while walking sends a lane-0 `RequestMode { TurnBased }`
   for the player's first controlled actor. The walk open binds the single
   player with `PauseRights { pause: true, unpause: true }` (the operator's
   rights in single-player; no other open changes). The host authorises,
   the sim applies the switch at the next round boundary and stops every
   route (fact 2). With the demo's `round_ticks: 10` the wait is at most
   0.5 s (`TICK_PERIOD_MS` 50, `crates/crpg-server/src/run.rs:30`). The client
   checks no side and no distance.
4. **"In a fight" is `view.active() != 0`.** No view column is added
   (fact 8). `ClientV4` already drops `ModeChanged`; the replica's turn fact
   is the signal.
5. **One demo hero, and a finished fight frees its survivors (D79b).** Fact
   3 makes a party of two fight on after the goblin dies, so the demo has one
   hero. Fact 4 then freezes the hero, so T157a and T157b change one
   predicate in two places: a participant is held by turn order only while
   its turn-based encounter **has a turn head** (`combat.active.is_some()`;
   `None` means terminal, `crates/crpg-sim/src/combat.rs:845–846`). A
   real-time terminal encounter already frees its survivors
   (`movement.rs:784–791` refuses turn-based only), so this makes the two
   modes agree. ADR-0036 M4 and W2 are amended (Manager edits). The two
   assertions of fact 4 change from `TurnBased` to the route checks; this is
   the rule changing by the user's decision, not a test weakened to pass, and
   needs the user's D79b answer.
6. **The demo data is a new campaign, `campaigns/demo/adventure`** (K-5): the
   walk campaign's area, ids and positions minus the second hero; a separate
   6-point movement pool `R` refilled each turn (6 m per turn at 1 m per
   point), beside the 1-point action pool Strike spends; Strike's reach
   1.5 m; `round_ticks: 10`; hero health 20 so the demo fight is not lost.
   `campaigns/demo/walk` and every fixture keep their bytes (walk.gd, rt.rs
   and the K-tests of T050 pin them).
7. **A turn-based ground click moves the active hero to the click; the host
   decides the rest.** Too far is refused (`CannotAfford` maps to
   `IllegalAction`, 18, `crates/crpg-server/src/host.rs:2024–2029`) and the
   HUD's existing receipt line shows it. No clamping, no preview, no
   movement-left readout (D79c, F-move-readout).
8. **One scene, a new one: `Adventure.tscn`, whose script extends
   `Walk.gd`.** `Walk.tscn` must keep two heroes for `walk.gd` (WK2, WK4),
   so the one-hero demo cannot be its default. `Adventure.gd` extends
   `res://scripts/Walk.gd` and overrides `_ready`, `_process`,
   `_unhandled_input` and `_campaign_dir`; `Walk.gd`, `Pick.gd` and
   `Feedback.gd` keep their bytes. `Main.tscn` stays the default scene and
   CI's smoke target (CI's client artifact ships only `combat_basic`,
   `.github/workflows/ci.yml:342`); making `Adventure.tscn` the default is
   follow-up F-default-scene (a workflow, S-set).
9. **Clicks are routed by the scene, not by `Pick`.** `Pick` is a child node
   whose input handler the scene switches off (`set_process_unhandled_input(false)`
   in `_ready`, which runs after the children's ready); the scene calls
   `Pick.pick_proxy` and `Pick.attack` itself. This settles T156's open
   "which handler wins" (`tasks/T156.md:209–212`) without touching `Pick.gd`.
10. **Nothing in the scene decides a rule.** It never checks sides, reach or
    allowance; the one `ended()` check only avoids sending a mode request the
    host would accept but never apply (fact 4).

### What follow-ups must not contradict
`open_v4_walk` gates the AI on turn-based turns only; an AI that acts in
real time needs a new `NpcDriver` variant (T156's note stands). A fight ends
only at fewer than two living combatants until F-sides; a party demo needs it.
`request_mode` sends for `controlled()[0]`; a session with no controlled
actor cannot request a mode.

## Behaviour
Normative. "Refused" means the stated code and no state change. **First
failure wins** in every list.

### T157a
S-1. **`World::set_route`** refuses `TurnBased { entity }` exactly when the
entity is a combatant and the encounter is turn-based **and has a turn head**:
`self.combatants().contains(entity) && self.combat().is_some_and(|state|
state.mode == EncounterMode::TurnBased && state.active.is_some())`. Every
other check and its order (`AbsentEntity`, `NotMover`, `DeadCombatant`,
`TurnBased`, then `validate_route`) is unchanged, so a survivor of a finished
turn-based fight sets routes as a real-time participant or a non-participant
does, and stage 4 moves it (ADR-0036 W1). The doc comment's last sentence
becomes: "A combatant of a real-time encounter, a survivor of a finished
(head-less) turn-based encounter and every non-participant may set a route."
`perform_move`, `validate_move`, `place`, `clear_route` and every other
function are unchanged. *Tests:* S1, S2.

S-2. **The two pinned assertions** change, and nothing else in their tests:
- `tests/movement_core.rs:928–931`: the comment becomes `// B is alive in the
  finished turn-based encounter: no turn holds it, so the route checks run
  (T157a).` and the expected value `Err(MoveError::TurnBased { entity: b })`
  becomes `Err(MoveError::StartMismatch)` (B stands at (10, 0, 0); the route
  starts at `p(0, 0)`), still inside `unchanged(..)`.
- `tests/movement_core_contract.rs:1194–1198`: the `unchanged_on_err(..)`
  assertion becomes `assert_eq!(w.set_route(b, route(2, vec![p(10, 0),
  p(10, 1)])), Ok(None));` with the comment `// B survives the finished
  fight: the route checks run and pass (T157a).` The next assertion
  (`set_route(c, ..) == Ok(Some(RouteTag(3)))`) is unchanged.
The `#[test]` and assert counts of both files do not fall.

### T157b
L-1. **`decide_move_outcome` step (1)** rejects `WrongMode` exactly when the
actor is a combatant and the encounter is turn-based **and has a turn head**
(`combat.active.is_some()`), the predicate of S-1. Everything after it is
unchanged: a survivor of a finished fight gets `MoveTo` planned and set
(`Accepted { start_tick: t + 1 }`) and `Halt` cleared (`Accepted`). The
comment above step (1) gains: "A finished turn-based fight holds no one
(T157b)." *Tests:* L1.

### T157c
K-1. **`open_v4_walk_with`** is `open_v4_rt_with` (W-1 of T050) with T156a's
`NpcDriver::ServerAi` branch (A-1 of T156) and the player bound with
`WALK_RIGHTS`. Argument checks, `AlreadyOpen`, campaign, side and start
failures are `open_v4_rt`'s, in its order and with its texts (a ruleset
without `round_ticks` is 25). `open_v4_walk` calls it with
`SystemClock::new()`. *Tests:* K1, K2, K3.

K-2. **Rights.** `open_v4_guarded` passes its new `rights` argument to
`bind_v4_peer`; nothing else uses it. `open`, `open_v4*`, `open_v4_rt*` and
`open_v4_ai*` bind exactly as before (`PauseRights::default()`), so every
existing session keeps its bytes. *Tests:* K1.
