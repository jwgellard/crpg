## Task

A playable slice in one Godot scene: a party of two heroes walks in real
time; a fight starts in place when a hero comes near the goblin (a server
rule) or when the player clicks the goblin; in the fight the player clicks
the floor to move the active hero within a per-turn allowance that the HUD
shows, and clicks the goblin to strike; when only the heroes' side is left
the fight ends and the party walks again. The enemy is played by the same
server AI as on the dedicated server, in the background host; the client
decides no rule. The user chose (D79): both ways to start (a), sides first
(b), the readout now (c).

What exists, found in code:

1. **No exploration without an encounter in a bridge session.** Every open
   starts the authored encounter in a chosen mode before the host exists
   (`crates/crpg-godot/src/core/mod.rs:769–772`), and `Host` has no start,
   end, join or leave operation (its public list,
   `crates/crpg-server/src/host.rs:979–5494`). Today's walk scene walks inside
   a real-time encounter (`apps/client/scripts/Walk.gd:32`).
2. **Real time to turn-based is an existing, host-authorised switch.** A
   `RequestMode` intent needs the `pause` right (`host.rs:6175–6176`); the sim
   sets `pending_mode` (`crates/crpg-sim/src/combat.rs:4366–4384`) and applies
   it at the next round boundary, stopping every route (`combat.rs:4469–4486`,
   `:4526–4537`). `Host::request_mode` is the host's own request
   (`host.rs:4508–4519`).
3. **The sim has no sides.** A fight ends when fewer than two combatants are
   alive (`combat.rs:3885–3898`); the same count gates real time
   (`rt_alive`, `:4346–4353`, used at `:2682`, `:3502`, `:3578`, `:4498`,
   `:4570`, `:4644`), the turn-based rollover (`:4240–4250`, `:4286–4294`), a
   leave or despawn (`crates/crpg-sim/src/world.rs:1150–1157`), and two host
   copies (`host.rs:4543–4549`, `crates/crpg-server/src/host/ai.rs:144–154`).
   Creature data already has `faction: Option<Ulid>`
   (`crates/crpg-data/src/document.rs:125`), with `crpg.faction/1` documents
   under `factions/` (`crates/crpg-data/src/loader.rs:124`) and reference
   checks (`crates/crpg-data/src/inventory.rs:753`); the sim never reads it.
   No campaign or sim, server or testkit fixture that starts an encounter
   sets a faction (only `crates/crpg-data/tests/fixtures/broken_references`
   names one).
4. **A finished turn-based fight freezes its survivors.** `set_route`
   refuses every participant of a turn-based encounter, terminal or not
   (`crates/crpg-sim/src/movement.rs:784–791`), pinned for a terminal one by
   `crates/crpg-sim/tests/movement_core.rs:928–931` and
   `crates/crpg-sim/tests/movement_core_contract.rs:1194–1198`; the host's
   lane-1 decision refuses them `WrongMode` first (`host.rs:4061–4068`); a
   pending switch back to real time never applies in a terminal encounter
   (`combat.rs:4372–4373`).
5. **The server AI plays turn-based turns and waits in real time.**
   `ServerAiHook` gates on the NPC peer having legal options
   (`crates/crpg-godot/src/core/authority/v4.rs:203`), which exist only for a
   turn head (`host.rs:1107–1117`). It gets its sides from the bridge's
   presentation strings (faction name, else creature name; `ai_sides`,
   `v4.rs:155–170`, from `authority.rs:432–450`).
6. **The embedded host binds a navigation mesh.** `bind_area_bounds` bakes
   the area box and stores it (`host.rs:4605`, `:4618–4628`); `drive_ai`
   passes it to `decide` (`host/ai.rs:213`, `:249`).
7. **Pool facts already travel.** A v3/v4 session's `FactGrants` may grant an
   entity's `pools` (`host.rs:270–292`); the host projects each combatant's
   pools under that grant (`crates/crpg-server/src/host_v3.rs:409–420`) and
   the replica keeps them (`FactSet.pools`,
   `crates/crpg-net/src/protocol_v3.rs:387–399`). The bridge binds the player
   with `FactGrants::default()` (all denied, `mod.rs:617`).
8. **The view has what the scene needs, except the pool.** `active()` is the
   turn head in turn-based mode and 0 otherwise (`client_v4.rs:475–480`);
   `declare_move` moves the current options' actor (`client_v4.rs:231–254`);
   refused moves reach the HUD log as receipts (`CombatHud.gd:147–149`,
   `:203–209`).

## Readings
Design decisions this contract makes. Each needs approval with the contract.

1. **A side is the creature's faction; no faction means a side of one**
   (ADR-0029 Amendment 1 S1). Faction is already authored data with
   reference checks (fact 3), so no new vocabulary is needed. Grouping
   faction-less creatures by creature type instead (what the HUD shows
   today) would change every recorded baseline of a multi-creature fight
   and need a re-baseline review; "a side of one" changes nothing that
   exists. Faction `relations` are not read: different factions are
   opposed (F-relations).
2. **A fight lasts while two sides stand** (S2). One sim predicate,
   `fight_is_live`, replaces every "two or more alive" test, in the sim and
   in the host's two copies, so the dedicated and embedded hosts agree.
3. **Engagement is the host's rule, parametrised by data** (S3, D79a (c),
   D80a). The notice distance is an optional encounter field; the sim gives
   the read-only `noticed(world)`; every host's `advance` calls it after its
   tick and makes its own mode request. The sim never chooses a mode. A
   player's click still requests the same switch early. "Enemy" is
   `opposed` (Reading 1).
4. **Survivors of a finished fight walk (D80b (a)).** Ending the encounter
   "properly" (the host releasing it) would turn survivors into creature
   records whose health and death the host does not project (only
   combatants carry health facts, `host_v3.rs:398–408`), so the bars and the
   corpse would vanish; that is F-lifecycle. The one-predicate change ("held
   while the turn-based encounter has a turn head") stays, in the sim and
   the host's lane-1 check, as ADR-0036 Amendment 1 says.
5. **Walking is the encounter's real-time mode; the AI waits in it** (as
   revision 1). The walk open reuses `ServerAiHook`'s turn-based gate on
   purpose (fact 5); an enemy that fights in real time is F-rt-ai. The open
   is named `open_v4_walk`.
6. **The AI's sides stay the bridge's for now.** With factions, the bridge's
   presentation side strings are faction names, which agree with the sim's
   sides whenever every combatant has a faction (the demo). Deriving the
   AI's sides from the sim in the host is the existing backlog row
   (`tasks/BACKLOG.md:156`, D78a), now unblocked: F-ai-sides. It changes
   `drive_ai`'s signature and two callers, so it is not small.
7. **The single player holds the pause rights and sees its own pools.** The
   walk open alone binds the player with `PauseRights { pause: true,
   unpause: true }` and with a `pools: true` fact grant for each of the
   player's actors; every other open binds as today.
8. **The readout is pool points of the ruleset's movement pool.** The view
   gains `movement()` and `movement_max()`: per row, the current and maximum
   of the pool the ruleset's `movement.turn.pool` names, from the disclosed
   facts, `-1` when not disclosed or when the ruleset has no movement. The
   client converts nothing (in the demo one point is one metre); a metres
   readout and a reach ring are F-move-metres.
9. **The demo data is `campaigns/demo/adventure`**: the walk room with both
   heroes (faction Heroes) and the goblin (faction Goblins); a 6-point
   movement pool refilled each turn beside the 1-point action pool; Strike's
   reach 1.5 m; `round_ticks: 10` (a switch waits at most 0.5 s); notice
   4 m; hero health 20.
10. **One scene, `Adventure.tscn`, whose script extends `Walk.gd`**; the
    scene routes clicks itself and switches `Pick`'s handler off; clicking
    your own hero in a fight never attacks it (the HUD's buttons still offer
    every legal option); `Main.tscn` stays the default and CI's smoke
    target (F-default-scene). `Walk.gd`, `Pick.gd`, `Feedback.gd`,
    `CombatHud.gd` keep their bytes.

### What follow-ups must not contradict
Every rule about the end of a fight goes through `fight_is_live`; nothing may
count living combatants against 2 again. Engagement runs only in `advance`
after a tick (not in `Host::tick`, the operator primitive). `noticed` is
read-only. `open_v4_walk`'s AI is turn-based only.

/// An encounter exists and its living combatants stand on two or more sides (S-A4).
pub fn fight_is_live(world: &World) -> bool;

## Behaviour
Normative. "Refused" means the stated code and no state change. **First
failure wins** in every list.

### T157a — sides
S-A1. **Fields.** `Combatant.faction` and `CreatureState.faction` as in the
Interface. A world in which every value is `None` serializes, hashes
(`state_hash`) and replays byte-identically to today.

S-A2. **Sources.** A spawned participant takes its prefab creature
document's `faction` (the spec's `creatures` map). `register_creature`
copies the creature document's `faction` into the record. A combatant made
from a record (enter, member, joiner) copies the record's; `exit_record`
(end, leave) copies the combatant's back. Nothing else writes either field.

S-A3. **`side_of(world, e)`**: a combatant → `Faction(f)` when its faction is
`Some(f)`, else `Alone(e)`; else a creature record, the same; else `None`.
**`opposed(world, a, b)`**: `a != b`, both sides `Some`, and they differ.

S-A4. **`fight_is_live(world)`**: `world.combat()` is `Some` and the set of
`side_of` over combatants that are not dead has at least two members. A
crate-private `live_sides(world) -> usize` gives that count.

S-A5. **Every "two alive" rule reads "two sides".** Each site of fact 3 in
the sim (`combat.rs:2682`, `:3502`, `:3578`, `:3885–3898`, `:4240–4250`,
`:4286–4294`, `:4346–4353` and its users `:4498`, `:4570`, `:4644`;
`world.rs:1150–1157`) compares `live_sides(world)` with 2 where it compared
a living count, and `rt_alive` is removed or becomes `live_sides`. Before
the edit, the implementer greps `crates/crpg-sim/src` for living counts
compared with 2 and lists every hit in the PR; any site beyond this list is
changed the same way and named. With no faction set, every result is
unchanged.

S-A6. **Nothing else changes**: no event, no history version, no error
variant, no hosted rule.
