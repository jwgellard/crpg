## Behaviour

E015 requires speculative state in a separate local movement buffer, outside
sim and the immutable authoritative replica. Reconcile at the acknowledged seq,
then replay newer local inputs using the approved movement rules. Server tick
is time, seq is identity; neither grants client scheduling authority. Never
predict attacks, damage, resources, turns or remote entity authority.

Remote entities interpolate only permitted samples. Old/out-of-order ack must
not regress accepted state; explicit resync/fenced epoch clears incompatible
buffered inputs. Define drop/overflow/resync policy before implementation. No
hidden entities can appear through interpolation history after revocation.
Spatial presentation smoothing cannot alter authoritative hashes or receipts.
