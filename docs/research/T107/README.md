## What "not found" means

A `not found` verdict means the checker could not locate the cited claim at the source. It does not mean the claim is wrong. `unavailable` marks a source that could not be fetched. `paraphrase` rows are left unmarked: the checker found the claim's substance in the source, but not the wording.
