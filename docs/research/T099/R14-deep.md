# R14-deep — Pause and time modes, single player and multiplayer (T099)

No pass 1 exists (stopped by a rate limit), so every recommendation is `new`.
Our setup: one server-authoritative `Timeline` queue advanced every 20 Hz tick (real time) or on `EndTurn` (turn-based).

## Recommendations (ranked by impact on our time model and multiplayer rules)

1. Make pause a host-granted, revocable permission; anyone holding it pauses everyone; split pause vs unpause rights. Applies: decides our pause authority on the 20 Hz server without trusting co-location. Evidence: https://pbg.bgforge.net/bg/help/multiplayer/host/index.html — "Pause: the ability to pause the game." (vendor doc) + https://www.gog.com/forum/baldurs_gate_series/how_to_multiplayer — "When a player pauses the game it's paused for everyone." (practitioner report) + https://forums.beamdog.com/discussion/2055/co-op-pausing — "give himself unpause privileges while giving everyone else pause privileges" (practitioner report). Effort: medium. `new`.
2. Support per-encounter on-the-fly RTwP-to-TB switching on the one Timeline; never lock mode per campaign. Applies: our Timeline already has two advance policies, so switching is a policy flip, not a second engine. Evidence: https://wrath.owlcat.games/news/16 — "You will be able to switch between modes on the fly, without the need to reload the location" (vendor doc) + https://www.pcgamer.com/pathfinder-wrath-of-the-righteous-will-have-both-real-time-and-turn-based-combat-modes/ — "fight any encounter in either mode at any moment" (practitioner report). Deadfire locked per campaign ("select the combat mode you wish your new game to stay in", https://eternity.obsidian.net/news/pillars-of-eternity-ii-deadfire-update-60---patch-41---turn-based-mode-beta, vendor doc) and later reversed it ("swap between real-time with pause and turn-based combat in the options menu", https://eternity.obsidian.net/news/turn-based-mode-public-beta-is-live, vendor doc). Effort: large. `new`.
3. Fix late-join initiative rules: joining in round 1 keeps a full turn slotted behind acted combatants; joining later skips the turn; same player's tied characters act simultaneously. Applies: split parties and co-op join mid-fight on our tick-driven Timeline. Evidence: https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Initiative — "Joining during the first round of combat guarantees that the joining character can take their full turn." + "Joining after the first round of combat causes joining participants to automatically skip their turn." + "characters controlled by the same player with the same Initiative effectively act simultaneously" (doc). Effort: medium. `new`.
4. Make auto-pause a server-side policy with per-trigger toggles, host-controlled, default-off in multiplayer. Applies: auto-pause is the top pause-spam vector on a shared timeline. Evidence: https://www.gog.com/forum/baldurs_gate_series/how_to_multiplayer — "Because everyone has different auto-pause settings, the auto-pause is disabled in multiplayer." (practitioner report) + https://sampsca.github.io/TobExReference.html — "allowing auto-pause to work on any screen in single player. The behavior mimics that of multiplayer." (doc) + NWN server setting "player pause allowed y/n?" (https://alt.games.neverwinter-nights.narkive.com/CQ2WecvQ/multiplayer-no-pause, practitioner report). Effort: small. `new`.
5. Add turn timers, delay/skip, and a shared visible order to TB; disable party AI in TB. Applies: multiplayer TB pacing complaints are all about waiting on slow turns. Evidence: https://www.d20pfsrd.com/gamemastering/Combat/ — "Five Second Rule: ... skip him as if he had chosen to delay his action" + "Display the Combat Order" (doc) + https://pillarsofeternity2.wiki.fextralife.com/Combat — "Party Member AI and Re-targeting are disabled." + "Magran's Challenge adds a time limit to turns intead of limiting pausing." (doc). Effort: small. `new`.
6. Split parties stay split: only the engaged group enters TB; the rest stay real-time and join on approach or attack. Applies: our Timeline must run both policies at once for different entities. Evidence: Divinity OS:EE manual — "the game will only be in turn-based mode for that character." (vendor doc, via https://cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/373420/manuals/DOSEE_Manual.pdf?t=1665155407) + https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Initiative — "the rest of the party can be moved into position to join the fight." (doc) + https://dlassets-ssl.xboxlive.com/public/content/6778dff3-8d6c-4615-a8a3-1f1770057d09/GameManual/9e540603-b9bb-44a0-83cd-bb98f1d40b32/en-US/index.html — "You can also split up the party and control them one by one" (vendor doc). Effort: large. `new`. [check: unavailable — V9]
7. Trivial dialogue and stores must not pause; important dialogue pauses with a party vote and host tiebreak. Applies: dialogue-pause is the second pause-spam vector after combat pause. Evidence: BG manual via https://forums.beamdog.com/discussion/7655/how-about-that-pause-dialog-option-multiplayer-bgee — "will not pause the game when spoken to... Other players will be able to continue playing." (vendor doc) + https://baldursgate3.wiki.fextralife.com/Multiplayer — "other party members will be able to vote" (doc) + Solasta co-op — "the host breaks any ties in dialog options." (https://www.reddit.com/r/CrownOfTheMagister/comments/xd84k4/quick_mp_question/, practitioner report). Effort: medium. `new`.
8. Design the wait out of TB: shared turn order, off-turn reactions and planning, fast animations; treat waits as social space. Applies: waiting complaints dominate TB multiplayer feedback. Evidence: https://www.mmi.ifi.lmu.de/pubdb/publications/pub/maekelae2020chiplay/maekelae2020chiplay.pdf — "turn-based systems were criticized for being slow and boring, mostly because of the high frequency and length of battles and the amount of waiting" (measured study) + https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/2c6bb612-f7bb-4375-906b-68269f16258e/content — "In cooperative multiplayer games, waiting situations can be used for planning and conversation" (paper) + https://boardgamedesigncourse.com/how-to-reduce-downtime-in-your-game/ — "Give players something to do when other players are taking their turn." (practitioner report). Effort: medium. `new`.
9. Large dedicated worlds get no global player pause; pause becomes a DM-only power, default-off. Applies: our "larger dedicated worlds" cannot freeze for one player's menu. Evidence: https://sorcerers.net/Games/NWN/faq2.php — "the game is being designed and balanced with non-pausing, real-time game play in mind." (vendor doc) + https://alt.games.neverwinter-nights.narkive.com/CQ2WecvQ/multiplayer-no-pause — "almost every PW disables pause and Time Stop." (practitioner report) + https://nwn.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeon_Master_client — "possess and control any monster or non-player character" (doc). Effort: small. `new`.
10. Keep the fixed 20 Hz step; TB is the same Timeline advanced on EndTurn; never use a variable step. Applies: confirms KERNEL's one-mechanism-two-policies design and determinism for the dry-run simulator. Evidence: https://gameprogrammingpatterns.com/game-loop.html — "We'll update the game using a fixed time step because that makes everything simpler and more stable" (book) + https://neverwintervault.org/sites/neverwintervault.org/files/project/4333/files/nwn2-gb.pdf — "A round lasts approximately six seconds" (vendor doc) + https://www.d20pfsrd.com/gamemastering/Combat/ — "Each round represents 6 seconds in the game world" (doc). Effort: small. `new`.
11. Never hard-eliminate a player from a session: spectatorship plus optional control of allied mobs. Applies: split-party and large fights will sideline players; idleness kills co-op. Evidence: https://www.cs.vu.nl/~eliens/rif/media/pattern-earlyelimination.html — "let the Eliminated players participate in the game in some manner, for example, as Spectators" (course notes) + https://tabletopgamesblog.com/2022/09/13/slow-death-player-elimination-in-board-games-topic-discussion/ — "allow players to continue playing by giving them a new role." (blog) + https://medium.com/@mrmatthew/designing-for-player-elimination-3795da8859b2 — "comfortable waiting between 5 and 14 minutes after being eliminated" (practitioner report). Effort: medium. `new`.
12. Plan the GM mode as possess-and-vignette on top of TB, capped at ~4 players, not as a second engine. Applies: our editor-as-GM-client direction matches Larian's proven split. Evidence: https://pressreleases.triplepointpr.com/2017/05/09/larian-studios-on-a-roll-reveals-game-master-mode-for-divinity-original-sin-2/ — "game masters can let enemies fight automatically, or take direct control over NPCs" (vendor doc) + https://docs.larian.game/GM_Frequently_Asked_Questions — "Divinity Engine 2 limits amount of player up to 4." (doc) + https://larian.com/support/faqs/what-is-a-game-master-mode_14 — "Game Master Mode allows you to play a tabletop role-playing session using our system!" (vendor doc). Effort: large. `new`. ## What the research literature says

- RT, TB and RTwP are perceived as three distinct categories; most players have a preference but 58.1% stay open to all styles; TB is loved for relaxed pacing, hated for waiting; RTwP is loved for pacing control. https://www.mmi.ifi.lmu.de/pubdb/publications/pub/maekelae2020chiplay/maekelae2020chiplay.pdf — "58.1% expressed openness to all combat styles" + "TB was uniquely appreciated for its relaxed gameplay" (CHI PLAY 2020 paper, measured study).
- Waiting in games spans "turn-based action, and cutscenes", drives both "anticipation, frustration, and boredom", and shapes decisions, not just feelings. https://doi.org/10.1145/3748613 (via https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3748613) — "Waiting situations in games can take place during, for instance, loading screens, turn-based action, and cutscenes." (paper). [check: unavailable — V9]
- Turn-taking chores (waiting, upkeep, rule enforcement) are the foundation of social play; players tolerate reasonable waits and use them to talk. https://doi.org/10.26503/dl.v2011i1.591 — "Board game players seemed to be comfortable with reasonable waiting times." + "chores (e.g. waiting for a turn, rule learning and enforcement, maneuvering physical objects)" (DiGRA 2011 paper). [check: unavailable — V9]
- Validated co-op metrics link design patterns to observed cooperation; skill gaps produce a distinct "Waiting for each other" behavior that "builds frustration". https://doi.org/10.1145/1753326.1753363 + thesis https://summit.sfu.ca/_flysystem/fedora/sfu_migrate/11576/etd6462_BAghabeigi.pdf — "One important problem with cooperative games is the gap between skills" + "Most of the time this builds frustration" (CHI 2010 paper + SFU thesis). [check: unavailable — V9]
- Co-op pairs learn by stopping to watch each other ("Stop & think") and knowledge stays distributed across players. https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/130587/ — "Stop & think ... was most commonly coded when one player would stop playing in order to observe the other." (CHI PLAY 2014 paper). [check: unavailable — V9]
- Turn-taking is explicitly negotiated; control does not equal power. https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/70636b6c-aac3-4f39-a2d9-9b44a6867c2a — "turn taking was an explicitly negotiated choice, and decision making power did not necessarily rely on who was holding the controller" (paper). [check: unavailable — V9]
- Co-op games that add players without adapting design leave them hardly interacting. https://dl.digra.org/index.php/dl/article/view/639 — "may lead to games where the players hardly interact with each other and with little to none benefit when another player is present." (DiGRA 2014 paper). [check: unavailable — V9]
- Elimination is "expulsion from the game world before the conclusion of play" and pairs dangerously with negotiation; digital games should import the social-risk handling. https://leonardo.info/LEA/perthDAC/SWoods_LEA160203.pdf — "elimination is the threat of expulsion from the game world before the conclusion of play." (paper). [check: unavailable — V9]
- Downtime is "The player cannot directly affect the outcome of the game"; too much ruins immersion, but acceptable doses build anticipation; game masters can observe and break it. https://www.cs.vu.nl/~eliens/rif/media/pattern-downtime.html — "Too large periods of Downtime make reasonable waiting times impossible and can ruin Immersion." (course notes, after Bjork/Holopainen).
- Players made temporary spectators by turn-taking still need public information to stay engaged. https://www.cs.vu.nl/~eliens/rif/media/pattern-spectators.html — "Players currently in the game can temporarily become Spectators by Ultra-Powerful Events or Turn Taking" (course notes).
- References followed: (a) Makela paper's subjects: BG multiplayer pause docs + DOS2 combat/initiative pages above; (b) Early Elimination pattern's cites: Downtime + Spectators patterns above; (c) El-Nasr paper's basis: Aghabeigi SFU thesis + Reuter collaborative-patterns paper above.

## Contradictions

- Auto-pause in MP: GOG regulars say it "is disabled in multiplayer" (https://www.gog.com/forum/baldurs_gate_series/how_to_multiplayer) while Beamdog veterans recommend shared auto-pause, e.g. "Enemy sighted" (https://forums.beamdog.com/discussion/2055/co-op-pausing). Resolution: server policy with host toggles (Rec 4).
- Downtime: Gnome Stew calls designer-caused downtime a failure ("If you created an RPG that fosters downtime, you screwed up.", https://gnomestew.com/a-look-at-downtime-in-rpgs-and-board-games/) while Xu shows waits support social play ("comfortable with reasonable waiting times", https://doi.org/10.26503/dl.v2011i1.591) and BGDC says co-op needs less mitigation ("co-op games already have built-in collaboration", https://boardgamedesigncourse.com/how-to-reduce-downtime-in-your-game/). Resolution: keep waits short and sociable (Rec 8). [check: unavailable — V9]
- Waiting feeling: Tepponen finds both "anticipation, frustration, and boredom" (https://doi.org/10.1145/3748613) while Aghabeigi finds waiting from skill gaps mostly "builds frustration" (https://summit.sfu.ca/_flysystem/fedora/sfu_migrate/11576/etd6462_BAghabeigi.pdf). Resolution: timers and catch-up for skill-gap waits, free waiting for social waits. [check: unavailable — V9]
- Mode design: Owlcat balances for RTwP and layers TB on top; Rogue Trader reversed it ("designed with TB in mind (unlike PF which was balanced around RtwP)", https://owlcat.games/news/92), and surveys put TB at "about 70% vs 30%" (same). Resolution: balance per encounter for both, since players mix modes ("RtwP for easier fights and TB for difficult fight", same).
- Turn order fairness: DOS2 alternates allies/enemies regardless of rolls ("the turn order alternates between Allies and Enemies", https://divinityoriginalsin2.wiki.fextralife.com/Combat) while BG3 uses strict d20 order with simultaneous same-player turns (https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Initiative). Either is defensible; pick one and show the order. [check: unavailable — V9]

## Not applicable

- X-COM's real-time Geoscape vs turn-based Battlescape split is single-player strategy-layer design, not party co-op timekeeping (https://gdcvault.com/play/1017808/Classic-Game-Postmortem-X-COM).
- DOS2 Arena PvP and BG deathmatch arena rules (no pausing) cover competitive modes we are not building (https://sampsca.github.io/TobExReference.html).
- MMO-scale portal-linked PW sharding is far beyond our co-op party + dedicated world scope (https://sorcerers.net/Games/NWN/faq6.php).
- Idle/social-network waiting mechanics (pay-to-skip timers) do not transfer to session-based tactical co-op (Tepponen survey scope).
- Tabletop free-form turn negotiation without a server clock does not transfer; our Timeline must arbitrate (https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/70636b6c-aac3-4f39-a2d9-9b44a6867c2a).
