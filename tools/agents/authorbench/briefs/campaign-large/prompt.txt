# Authoring task

You are building game content for a CRPG engine. Work only inside this directory.

## What to build

Build a new campaign named `ashen-reach` from nothing, in `campaign/`. It has one world with two areas, `start` and `ridge`. Its rule system is your own, named `focus-d6`: three attributes (might, guile, resolve) and a health stat; a creature has one action each turn. It has four creatures, a hero, a goblin, an archer and a wolf; place the hero, the goblin and the archer in `start` and the wolf in `ridge`. It has two abilities. `strike` costs one action, rolls two six-sided dice against the attacker's might, and on a success deals 2 damage. `focus` costs one action, deals no damage, and on a success gives its user an effect named `focusing`, which lasts two turns and adds 2 to its bearer's might. Each ability has its own outcome table, `strike-table` and `focus-table`. It has two encounters: `skirmish`, in `start`, in which the hero, the goblin and the archer fight, and `ridge-ambush`, in `ridge`, in which the hero and the wolf fight. It has an item, `healing-draught`, and a faction, `vale-folk`, that the hero and the archer belong to. It has a dialogue, `elder-greeting`, of at least three nodes that ends, and a quest, `clear-the-ridge`, with at least two states, the last of them terminal. Either encounter may be the one that starts first; it must start with at least two combatants.

## Rules of the exercise

- Use only `crpgc` and the files in `schemas/`. Do not look for other documentation and do not read anything outside this directory.
- Put the finished campaign in the `campaign/` directory here. Every document is a JSON file.
- Check your work with `crpgc validate campaign --json` and keep going until it exits 0.
- Then check that it plays: give `crpgc play --campaign campaign` its choices on standard input, one number per line.
- When you are finished, reply with the single word DONE. If you cannot finish, reply with the word STUCK and one sentence saying why.
