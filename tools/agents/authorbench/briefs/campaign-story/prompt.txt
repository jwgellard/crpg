# Authoring task

You are building game content for a CRPG engine. Work only inside this directory.

## What to build

Build a new campaign named `quiet-ford` from nothing, in `campaign/`. It has one world and one area with the slug `start`. Its rule system is your own, named `plain-d6`: three attributes (might, guile, resolve) and a health stat; a creature has one action each turn. It has three creatures, a hero, a ferryman and a wolf, each placed in the area. It has one ability, `strike`, that costs one action; a strike rolls two six-sided dice against the attacker's might and succeeds when the roll is no higher than the might, a success deals 2 damage and a failure deals none, and the ability has its own outcome table, `strike-table`. It has a dialogue, `ferryman-talk`, in which the ferryman asks the hero for help: the hero's choice "I will deal with the wolf" starts the quest `wolf-hunt`, while "Not today" ends the talk. The quest `wolf-hunt` has three states: not started, started and completed. It has an event graph: when the wolf dies, if `wolf-hunt` is started, it becomes completed. It has one encounter, `wolf-fight`, in which the hero and the wolf fight. The fight must start with two combatants.

## Rules of the exercise

- Use only `crpgc` and the files in `schemas/`. Do not look for other documentation and do not read anything outside this directory.
- Put the finished campaign in the `campaign/` directory here. Every document is a JSON file.
- Check your work with `crpgc validate campaign --json` and keep going until it exits 0.
- Then check that it plays: give `crpgc play --campaign campaign` its choices on standard input, one number per line.
- When you are finished, reply with the single word DONE. If you cannot finish, reply with the word STUCK and one sentence saying why.
